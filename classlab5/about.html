About Lab5

Learning how to design beautiful, responsive websites inspired by nature and creativity.

Our Purpose

Lab5 focuses on building web pages that are both functional and visually engaging. In this project, we explore how HTML and CSS can create modern layouts, structured content, and user-friendly designs.

Inspired by the calm beauty of nature, the design uses natural colors, soft shadows, and clean spacing to make the content feel welcoming and easy to read.

Each card and section reflects the idea of learning digital design through simple, practical projects that connect creativity with real web development skills.
